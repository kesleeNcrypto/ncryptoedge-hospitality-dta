# Benchmark: ISO/IEC 27001

## Reference

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 specifies requirements for an information security management system (ISMS). Use the official standard and its applicable amendments for authoritative requirements. This checklist is not a substitute for the standard, an audit, or certification.

## Hospitality assessment topics

Review, as relevant:

- ISMS scope, context, interested parties, leadership, objectives, and continual improvement.
- Risk assessment and treatment methodology; Statement of Applicability and control rationale.
- Asset, identity, supplier, cloud, vulnerability, logging, incident, continuity, and change-management controls.
- Protection of guest and workforce data and systems supporting property operations.
- Internal audit, management review, corrective action, and evidence of operating effectiveness.

Use Annex A as a reference set in the context of risk treatment; do not assume every control applies identically or that a checklist alone establishes conformity.

## Evidence examples

Approved ISMS scope, risk register, treatment plan, Statement of Applicability, policies, access reviews, supplier assessments, incident records, exercise results, audit reports, and corrective-action tracking.

## Assessment caveat

Do not state “ISO 27001 compliant” or “certified” without appropriate, current evidence and a properly scoped independent certification where that claim is made. Verify standard edition, amendments, scope, exclusions, and certificate status.

## Starting points

- ISO: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 should be obtained from an authorized standards source.
