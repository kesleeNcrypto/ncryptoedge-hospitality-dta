# Benchmark: ECOWAS and hospitality considerations

## Purpose and applicability

ECOWAS instruments and regional initiatives may inform privacy, cybersecurity, digital trade, travel, and cross-border operations. They do not replace the national laws and regulators applicable in each country. Hospitality obligations can also arise from tourism, accommodation, consumer protection, employment, payment, immigration, safety, and local licensing rules.

## Assessment approach

For every country in scope:

1. Identify the operating entity, properties, systems, data subjects, and cross-border flows.
2. Map applicable national privacy/data-protection laws, regulators, cybersecurity obligations, and breach procedures.
3. Check tourism/accommodation licensing, guest registration, consumer disclosure, accessibility, health and safety, and employment requirements.
4. Identify payment-card and acquiring-bank obligations where cardholder data or payment services are involved.
5. Review vendor locations, regional hosting, support access, and transfer arrangements.
6. Track differences between jurisdictions rather than assuming a single regional rule.

## Evidence examples

Country-by-country legal register, licenses, privacy notices, data-flow map, transfer records, processor contracts, payment scope documentation, incident procedures, and local counsel review.

## Assessment caveat

This is a scoping aid, not an exhaustive statement of ECOWAS law or a determination that a regional instrument applies. Confirm current instruments, ratification and implementation status, and country-specific requirements through authoritative sources and qualified local counsel.

## Starting points

- ECOWAS official website: https://www.ecowas.int/
- Relevant national data-protection, tourism, cybersecurity, and consumer-protection authorities in each country of operation.
