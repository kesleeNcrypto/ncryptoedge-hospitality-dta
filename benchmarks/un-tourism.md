# Benchmark: UN Tourism

## Purpose

Use UN Tourism materials as non-binding international context for responsible, sustainable, inclusive, and resilient tourism. UN Tourism guidance does not substitute for applicable laws, technical security standards, or contractual obligations.

## Hospitality assessment topics

Consider whether digital services and data practices support:

- Trust, transparency, accessibility, and respectful treatment of guests and workers.
- Responsible use of guest data, personalization, and automated decision support.
- Inclusion of users with disabilities, language needs, and limited digital access.
- Resilience of services important to travel, accommodation, and guest safety.
- Responsible supplier relationships and sustainability objectives where in scope.

## Evidence examples

Accessibility reviews, guest communications, complaint and escalation routes, responsible-data policies, supplier criteria, resilience plans, and documented impact reviews.

## Assessment caveat

Use the specific UN Tourism publication or instrument relevant to the engagement and record its title, version, date, and status. This summary does not claim that a particular UN Tourism guideline is binding or exhaustive.
