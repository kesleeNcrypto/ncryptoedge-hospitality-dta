# Benchmark: Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA)

## Purpose and applicability

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 establishes Nigeria’s data-protection framework and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Assess applicability to the relevant controller/processor roles, processing activities, individuals, and territorial connections. Confirm current subsidiary instruments, guidance, and any sector-specific requirements with qualified counsel and authoritative sources.

## Hospitality assessment topics

Consider, as applicable:

- Guest, employee, visitor, and loyalty-program personal-data inventories and purposes.
- Lawful basis, transparency, privacy notices, consent where relied upon, and handling of individual rights.
- Data minimization, accuracy, retention, deletion, and access controls.
- Controller/processor arrangements, vendor instructions, and international data transfers.
- Security measures, incident response, and breach assessment/notification processes.
- Governance responsibilities, records, risk assessments, and Data Protection Officer or other oversight requirements where applicable.
- Sensitive personal data and processing that may create elevated risk.

## Evidence examples

Privacy notices; records of processing; retention schedules; data-flow maps; processor agreements; rights-request logs; transfer assessments; incident procedures; training records; and security-risk assessments. Do not place guest data or breach details in this public-facing benchmark document.

## Assessment caveat

This is a non-exhaustive issue-spotting checklist, not a legal interpretation or compliance determination. Verify current law, NDPC guidance, applicable thresholds, and deadlines for the specific entity and processing.

## Authoritative starting points

- Nigeria Data Protection Commission: https://ndpc.gov.ng/
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (official publication should be confirmed through Nigerian government sources before reliance).
