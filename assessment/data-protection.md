# Data protection assessment

## Scope

Map personal-data processing for guests, employees, applicants, visitors, and loyalty members, including data handled by booking engines, property-management systems, payment providers, customer relationship platforms, analytics, support services, and AI vendors.

## Review objectives

- Identify data categories, purposes, collection points, recipients, locations, owners, and retention.
- Confirm notices, applicable legal basis, consent handling where relevant, and rights request processes.
- Review minimization, accuracy, access, retention, deletion, and safeguards for sensitive data.
- Assess processor contracts, vendor access, onward sharing, and international transfers.
- Review privacy risk assessments, governance roles, training, and records of processing where required.
- Examine incident detection, escalation, assessment, documentation, and notification decision processes.
- Consider privacy-by-design in new systems, loyalty features, personalization, and analytics.

## Evidence to request

Data maps and inventories; privacy notices; retention schedules; rights request procedures and aggregate logs; processor agreements; transfer records; risk assessments; access controls; training materials; and incident procedures.

## Data handling

Do not copy guest records, identity documents, payment-card data, or sensitive personal information into assessment materials. Use redacted or synthetic examples. Record evidence metadata and restricted references in the evidence register. Applicable requirements vary by jurisdiction; obtain qualified legal advice where needed.
