# Infrastructure assessment

## Scope

Tailor this guide to the approved environment. Hospitality infrastructure may include cloud tenants, property networks, point-of-sale and payment segments, property-management systems, guest Wi-Fi, building-management devices, backup services, identity platforms, and vendor-managed infrastructure. Inclusion does not authorize access or testing.

## Review objectives

- Establish asset ownership, business criticality, data sensitivity, and dependencies.
- Review identity, privileged access, authentication, remote access, and service accounts.
- Understand network segmentation, cloud configuration, endpoint protection, and administrative interfaces.
- Review vulnerability, patch, configuration, change, and software-supply-chain processes.
- Assess logging, monitoring, incident escalation, backup integrity, restoration, and continuity.
- Review supplier access, hosting, support, and shared-responsibility boundaries.
- Consider operational technology and safety impacts; avoid changes to production or building systems.

## Evidence to request

Current inventories and architecture diagrams; identity/access review records; configuration baselines; patch and vulnerability reports; change tickets; security monitoring coverage; incident procedures; backup and recovery test results; supplier assurance and access records; and continuity exercises.

## Assessment notes

Record scope and sampling, exceptions, evidence IDs, control status, and limitations. Do not collect credentials, exploit systems, scan production, or modify configurations unless separately and explicitly authorized with approved safeguards.
