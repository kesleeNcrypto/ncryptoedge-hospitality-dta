# AI governance assessment

## Scope

Inventory AI and automated decision features used in guest services, staff operations, marketing, security, pricing, translation, identity, and back-office workflows. Include embedded vendor features, pilots, and employee use of public generative-AI services.

## Review objectives

- Record system/provider, purpose, users, data, deployment context, version, owner, and affected people.
- Identify applicable legal roles and assess risk classification with qualified legal and technical input.
- Review data provenance, privacy and security, vendor terms, retention, and restrictions on reuse.
- Assess accuracy, robustness, bias and accessibility considerations proportionate to impact.
- Define human review, escalation, guest disclosure, contestability, and fallback procedures.
- Govern prompts and outputs; prevent unauthorized submission of guest, employee, payment, or confidential data.
- Monitor performance, drift, complaints, incidents, changes, and vendor updates.
- Maintain approval, testing, documentation, and retirement processes.

## Evidence to request

AI inventory; use-case approvals; vendor documentation and contracts; risk/classification assessments; data-flow records; testing and monitoring evidence; user notices; human-oversight procedures; and incident/escalation records.

## Caution

Do not infer legal classification from a product label or marketing description. The EU AI Act and other laws may apply depending on facts and jurisdiction. This guide is not authorization to submit data to an AI service or to make automated decisions about guests or staff.
