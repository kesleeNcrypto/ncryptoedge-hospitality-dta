# Evidence standard

## Purpose

Set minimum expectations for evidence used to support assessment observations and conclusions. Evidence must be relevant, attributable, sufficiently current, and handled safely.

## Evidence quality

Assess evidence for:

- **Relevance:** directly supports the control or condition being assessed.
- **Authenticity and provenance:** source, owner, collection method, and date are known.
- **Completeness:** scope, population, period, and exceptions are clear enough to interpret.
- **Currency:** evidence reflects the assessment period or its continuing validity is explained.
- **Corroboration:** important claims are supported by more than one suitable source where practical.
- **Integrity:** records have not been altered in a way that undermines their reliability.

## Evidence types

Identify the source type in notes: configuration or system record; policy/procedure; ticket or log; test or exercise result; contract or vendor assurance; interview; observation; or management attestation. Interviews and attestations can guide work but should not be represented as independently verified technical operation.

## Collection and minimization

- Use approved, access-controlled storage and transfer methods.
- Prefer redacted screenshots, sanitized exports, synthetic examples, and aggregate metrics.
- Do not place passwords, API keys, private keys, session tokens, payment-card data, or unnecessary personal data in this repository or register.
- Record evidence metadata and a restricted storage reference, not the underlying sensitive artifact.
- Capture only the minimum content necessary to substantiate the assessment.

## Evidence references

Assign stable IDs such as `EV-001`. Record title, type, custodian, collection date, coverage period, scope, source/reference, sensitivity, access location, and limitations in the evidence register. Link findings to evidence IDs. Keep evidence separate from report drafts where possible.

## Gaps and conflicting evidence

Record unavailable, incomplete, stale, or contradictory evidence explicitly. Seek clarification and corroboration. Do not infer that a control is absent solely because a document was not supplied. If uncertainty remains, use “not evidenced” and describe the assurance limitation.

## Retention and disposal

The engagement owner must set retention, access, and secure-disposal requirements in advance, consistent with contract and applicable law. Restrict access to named authorized personnel and maintain a record of material transfers or access where required.
