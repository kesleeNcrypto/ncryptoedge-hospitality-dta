# Remediation roadmap template

> Populate from validated findings. Owners and dates are agreed with management; risk acceptance is not an assessor decision.

## Prioritization principles

1. Address credible immediate threats to safety, guest privacy, payment security, and essential service availability.
2. Contain exposure before pursuing long-term redesign where appropriate.
3. Prioritize shared root causes and systemic controls over isolated symptoms.
4. Sequence work by risk, dependencies, operational windows, vendor commitments, and available resources.
5. Track residual risk, evidence of closure, and any approved exception with an expiry/review date.
