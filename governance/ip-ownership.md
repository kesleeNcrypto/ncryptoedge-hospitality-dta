# Intellectual property and deliverable ownership

> Confirm these terms in the executed engagement agreement. This checklist is not a contract or legal advice.

Document clearly:

- Ownership of each party’s pre-existing tools, templates, methods, software, and know-how.
- Ownership of assessment-specific reports, matrices, configurations, scripts, and other deliverables.
- Licenses needed for the client to use, modify, reproduce, and share deliverables internally or with advisers.
- Restrictions on external publication, marketing, benchmarking, and use of names or logos.
- Treatment of third-party materials and open-source components, including their license terms.
- Rights in anonymized or aggregated findings and safeguards against re-identification.
- Whether reusable methods or general skills may be retained, excluding confidential information and client data.
- Ownership, access, return, and deletion of source evidence and client-provided materials.

No assessment material should be published, reused as a case study, or distributed beyond approved recipients without written permission and appropriate review.
