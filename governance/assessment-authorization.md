## Minimum safeguards

- Obtain explicit written permission from the authorized system owner for each in-scope environment.
- Use least privilege, approved accounts, and synthetic or redacted data where practicable.
- Do not access, copy, alter, delete, or disclose out-of-scope information.
- Do not perform destructive actions, denial-of-service activity, persistence, social engineering, or production changes unless separately and specifically authorized with safeguards.
- Stop immediately on unexpected service impact, sensitive-data exposure, scope ambiguity, or a safety concern; notify the designated contact.
- Record activity and evidence securely, and report suspected incidents through the agreed channel.
