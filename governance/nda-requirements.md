# NDA and confidentiality requirements

> Working checklist for counsel and the engagement owner; not a legal agreement or legal advice.

Before sharing confidential information or beginning assessment work, establish written terms that address:

- Parties, authorized representatives, purpose, and assessment scope.
- Definition and permitted use of confidential information, including derived notes and reports.
- Permitted recipients, subcontractors, need-to-know access, and equivalent confidentiality duties.
- Approved storage, transfer, encryption, access logging, and incident notification expectations.
- Restrictions on copying, disclosure, publicity, and use of names or logos.
- Handling of personal data, security-sensitive information, credentials, and regulated records.
- Compelled disclosure procedure, exclusions, and duration of confidentiality obligations.
- Retention, return, and secure destruction, including backups and legal holds.
- Ownership and permitted use of pre-existing materials, work product, and de-identified learnings.
- Applicable law, dispute process, and relationship to the main services agreement.

Do not treat an NDA as authorization to access systems, test security, process personal data, or change production. Obtain separate written authorization and rules of engagement.
