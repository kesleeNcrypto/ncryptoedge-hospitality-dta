# NcryptoEdge Hospitality DTA

A structured, evidence-led framework for assessing digital technology, cybersecurity, privacy, and AI governance in hospitality environments.

> **Status:** Initial assessment framework. No organization or system has been assessed, and no compliance or security conclusion is implied by these materials.

## Purpose

This repository provides a repeatable starting point for a Digital Technology Assessment (DTA) covering hospitality operations such as booking and property-management systems, guest-facing applications, payment integrations, identity and access, cloud infrastructure, operational technology, and AI-enabled services.

## Repository map

- `methodology/` — assessment process, scoring, and evidence standards.
- `benchmarks/` — reference frameworks and regulatory topics to consider.
- `assessment/` — assessment domains and working matrix.
- `evidence/` — controlled evidence register.
- `reporting/` — report and remediation-plan templates.
- `governance/` — authorization, confidentiality, and intellectual-property controls.

## Getting started

1. Confirm the assessment sponsor, scope, locations, systems, dates, and exclusions.
2. Obtain written authorization before accessing any environment or data.
3. Agree on evidence handling, confidentiality, retention, and escalation procedures.
4. Tailor the benchmarks to the entity, jurisdictions, services, and contractual obligations.
5. Record evidence and findings in the controlled assessment records; do not infer implementation from policy statements alone.
6. Validate factual accuracy with system owners and qualified legal/privacy advisers before issuing conclusions.

## Assessment principles

- **Authorized and bounded:** perform only activities explicitly approved in writing and within agreed limits.
- **Evidence-led:** distinguish verified evidence, interviews, self-attestations, and unverified assumptions.
- **Risk-based:** prioritize realistic impact to guests, staff, operations, safety, and regulated data.
- **Data-minimizing:** use synthetic or redacted data wherever practicable; avoid collecting guest records or credentials.
- **No implied certification:** this framework is not a certification, penetration-test report, legal opinion, or guarantee of compliance.

## Handling and ownership

Treat assessment materials and evidence as confidential by default. Apply the organization’s approved access, storage, encryption, retention, and disposal controls. Confirm ownership and permitted reuse in the applicable agreement before sharing or publishing deliverables.

## Disclaimer

The benchmark documents are orientation aids, not legal advice or exhaustive statements of law. Applicability depends on facts including establishment, data subjects, processing, sector, contracts, and implementation dates. Confirm current requirements with qualified counsel and authoritative sources before relying on them.
